How to play

Fill the grid so every row, every column and every 3×3 box contains the digits 1 through 9 exactly once.

Tips

  • Turn on Notes (or hold Shift) to pencil in candidates.
  • Candidates turns auto-candidate mode on: every legal note is filled in and kept up to date as you play. Turn it off to get your own notes back.
  • Hint reveals the selected cell, or a random one if nothing is selected.
  • Your progress saves automatically. Pick up any day from the Archive.

Difficulty

Easy puzzles fall to singles. Medium ones need pairs and pointing. Hard ones usually need advanced techniques such as X-Wings or chains.

Puzzles are from The New York Times daily Sudoku, collected by the open-source nyt-sudoku-archive project.
